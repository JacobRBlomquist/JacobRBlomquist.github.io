Spring - Where It All Began

Like flowers blooming in the morning dew,
Our love began fresh, tender, and true.
Each laugh, each glance, each moment we'd share,
Planted seeds of love beyond compare.

In spring we met, in spring we grew,
Two hearts becoming one, me and you.

Summer - Love in Full Bloom

Though miles apart, our hearts stayed near,
Through every sunset, every tear.
The ring that bound us, promise true,
Until the day I'd marry you.

Summer's warmth could not compare
To love that conquered distance there.

Fall - Growing Our Family

Like autumn leaves that dance and fall,
We learned that love encompasses all.
New chapters written, dreams take flight,
Growing together, day and night.

In harvest time we reaped the best:
A family formed, our hearts blessed.

Winter - Seven Years Strong

Through winter's quiet, peaceful days,
We've learned love works in countless ways.
Seven years of joy and tears,
Growing stronger through the years.

Like evergreens that never fade,
Our love's foundation has been made.
